## Editorial Bar

Every rewritten sentence must pass at least one test:

- It clarifies what Wafia does.
- It makes the service feel more complete.
- It reduces perceived risk.
- It names a concrete deliverable or mechanism.
- It increases premium perception without sounding boastful.

Avoid filler: "premium", "data-driven", "best", "cheap", "real results", "zero risk", "no bullshit", "powerhouse".
